The Orbit Philosophy

Precision in Every Pour

Orbit was founded on a singular premise: that the alchemy of specialty coffee requires equal parts scientific precision and culinary intuition. From direct micro-lot partnerships in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Yemen to meticulous refractometer analysis, every cup is calibrated to highlight distinct terroir and elevated tasting profiles.
